MUSIC PLAYER FOR MAC
====================

The same player as on the PC - library, playlists, search, themes,
visualizer, lyrics, adding songs to your library - for your Mac.

On a Mac it runs inside Google Chrome (or Microsoft Edge or Brave), in a
window of its own with its own icon. Apple Music's full songs only play in a
real browser on the Mac, so that is what it uses.


SETTING IT UP (once)
--------------------
1. Copy this whole "Music Player for Mac" folder to the Mac
   (AirDrop, a USB stick, iCloud Drive ...). Put it anywhere, e.g. Documents.

2. Make sure Google Chrome is installed on the Mac (or Microsoft Edge / Brave).
   Get Chrome from https://www.google.com/chrome/ if not.

3. Open Terminal (press Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Return).
   Type  bash  and a space, then drag the file  install.sh  from this folder
   into the Terminal window, and press Return.

4. Music Player opens. With Edge or Brave the player is there straight away.
   With Google Chrome it comes together with Chrome's Extensions page and a
   window with directions, the first time. Do what it says once:
     - switch on "Developer mode" (top right of the Extensions page)
     - click "Load unpacked"
     - press Cmd + Shift + G, paste
           ~/Library/Application Support/Music Player/extension
       press Return, click "Select"
     - close the Extensions page, press Cmd + R in the Music Player window

5. Sign in to Apple Music in the Music Player window (the OPEN APPLE MUSIC key,
   then Apple's own Sign In button).

From then on just open "Music Player" - it is in the Applications folder in
your home folder, in Launchpad and in Spotlight. Drag it to the Dock.

The player has a browser profile of its own: its own sign-in and settings,
apart from your everyday browsing. (With Brave, Widevine - which Apple
Music's full songs need - is switched on in it from the start.)


MUSIC PLAYER IN THE DOCK
------------------------
Music Player stays open as long as the player is, so the Dock shows the dot
under it, like under any open app.
- Click it (or pick it with Cmd + Tab) and the player comes to the front -
  out of the Dock if it was minimized, opened again if its window was closed.
- Quit it (right-click it in the Dock, Quit) and the player quits too.
  Quit the player (Cmd + Q in its window) and Music Player goes too.
- The browser the player runs in shows in the Dock as well, with its own
  icon: it is a second copy of that browser, the player's own.


THE COMPACT PLAYER FLOATS ON TOP
-------------------------------
Press COMPACT (or Ctrl + M) and the small player opens in a little window of
its own that stays above every other window - Brave, Safari, Finder, any app -
so you can keep it in a corner while you do other things. Drag it wherever you
like; drag its edge to resize it.
- The tab it came from shows "FLOATING ON TOP" meanwhile.
- LIBRARY on the small player (or closing it, or its "back to tab" button)
  brings the full player back.
- The pin key on the small player switches the floating off and on again
  (it is the "Keep on top" setting under THEMES). With it off, the compact
  player is the browser window made small, as before.
- Needs Chrome, Edge or Brave 116 or later (2023 on). The browser only opens
  such a window right after a click or a key press, so when "Shrink to the
  compact player when music starts" kicks in a while after your click, the
  compact player may come up in the browser window instead.


THE VISUALIZER HEARS THE MUSIC
------------------------------
The page cannot hear Apple's songs (they are copy-protected), but the browser
lets the Music Player extension listen to the tab and pass the sound on:
- Started from the "Music Player" app: it just works.
- In your everyday browser (the extension loaded there with "Load unpacked"):
  click the Music Player button in the toolbar once, while the Apple Music tab
  is in front - each time Apple Music is opened. (Not pinned? It is in the
  puzzle-piece menu next to the address bar; the pin there keeps it in view.)
  Until then the visualizer dances to a built-in beat, says DEMO, and shows a
  reminder. The button shows ON while it listens.
- While it listens, the browser marks the tab as being captured, and the sound
  is played on to your speakers by the extension. Nothing leaves your Mac.
  If playing it on ever fails, the listening stops and the tab plays the
  sound itself again. After a minute of silence (music paused) it stops by
  itself, and starts again when the music does.


WHAT IS DIFFERENT FROM THE PC
-----------------------------
- While the player is open the Dock shows the browser's icon next to Music
  Player's (see MUSIC PLAYER IN THE DOCK).
- Closing the player's window stops the music, but Music Player stays open,
  like any Mac app: click it to open the player again, or quit it.
- Adding songs to your library or playlists works the same and shows up on
  your phone and your PC too.


UPDATING
--------
Copy the newer "Music Player for Mac" folder over this one and run install.sh
again (step 3). If the player is open, install.sh closes it first, and opens
it again at the end. Your sign-in and settings stay. If Chrome asks you to
switch the extension on again, repeat step 4.
Coming from a version before this one (before November 2026)? Sign in to
Apple Music once more, in the Music Player window: until now a mix-up of two
names on the Mac's disk kept the player from getting a profile of its own,
so it ran in your everyday browser instead.
Using the extension in your everyday browser instead? After changing its
files, press the reload arrow on the Music Player card on the Extensions page
(brave://extensions or chrome://extensions), then reload the Apple Music tab.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- "Music Player" says something has moved: run install.sh again.
- The window shows plain Apple Music, not the player: the extension is not
  switched on - do step 4 again, then Cmd + R.
- Songs do not play: make sure you are signed in (step 5) and that your
  Apple Music subscription is active.
- To remove it: delete "Music Player" from your Applications folder and the
  folder ~/Library/Application Support/Music Player
